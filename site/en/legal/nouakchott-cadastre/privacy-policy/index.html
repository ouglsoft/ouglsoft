This policy describes how the app “Nouakchott Land Parcel Map” (the “App”) handles data. The App is developed and owned by El Ougl Software SARL (the “Company”, “we”). By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read this policy.

1Data stored locally on your device

  • The parcel and urban-planning database: the App’s own data and not user data, copied from the App package into its private space on first launch.
  • Your settings: language, imagery style, parcel shading intensity, neighbourhood order, display and multiple-selection options, and whether the location button is shown.
  • Favourites: the identifiers of the parcels you save.
  • The last selected area, if you enable the option to open it at startup.

The App does not keep a search history and does not store your location.

This data stays on your device until you edit or delete it, clear the App’s data or uninstall the App; this may cause it to be lost. The Company cannot access your locally stored data and does not view it, and cannot restore it if it is lost. Local storage does not protect your data from someone who has access to your device.

2External services (separate from local storage)

  • Esri (ArcGIS World Imagery): satellite imagery comes from Esri’s servers over the internet. Your device requests the map tiles needed for the displayed area; the requests include the tile coordinates, zoom level, network address (IP), technical connection information and an access key issued to the Company by Esri, which may allow the provider to learn the area you are viewing on the map. The provider processes these requests under its own terms and policy. The parcel database is not downloaded from the internet.
  • Browser: the privacy-policy and terms pages, and any external link, open in the browser and are subject to the policy of the site opened.

These services are subject to their own terms and privacy policies, and we give no warranty as to their accuracy, safety or continuity.

3Location

If you use the “My location” button, the App requests precise-location permission to show your position on the map and sort neighbourhoods by proximity, and accepts a position only if its accuracy is sufficient. It does not store your location, send it to the Company or track it in the background. Because the map is loaded from Esri, tile requests may reflect the area you move to on the map, including your area if you centre the view on it. You can revoke the permission at any time in the system settings.

4Favourites and sharing

Favourites keep on your device the identifiers of the parcels you select; the Company cannot access them and does not view them. If you share parcel details through the system share menu, or copy its coordinates to the clipboard, that information may pass to the app or person you select, and the clipboard may be readable by other apps depending on the Android version; the recipient’s policy then applies.

5Permissions

  • Internet: loading satellite imagery and opening links.
  • Precise and approximate location (optional): the “My location” feature and sorting neighbourhoods by proximity only. Manual search remains available without it.

The App does not request camera, microphone, contacts or messaging access.

6Android backup

Android backup and data transfer are enabled for the App; its settings and favourites may be copied according to your device settings and the service you choose, and its policy. The parcel database is excluded.

7Your choices

You can manage the location permission, delete your favourites, disable opening the last area, clear the App’s data or uninstall it, and turn off backup in your device settings.

8Security and children

Protection of locally stored data depends on the App’s design and Android’s security controls; no system is completely secure, and you are responsible for protecting your device. The App is not directed at children.

9Changes and contact

We may amend this policy when data processing or legal requirements change; the amended text is usually published with its update date. When you write to us, we process your email address, your message and any attachments you send to the extent necessary to handle your request; contacting us creates no obligation to reply or to answer your questions.

10Summary

  • The App does not send user data to the Company, which cannot access it, holds no copy of it and cannot restore it if it is lost.
  • It requires no account or sign-in.
  • It contains no advertising and no analytics tools.
  • The parcel and plan database is built into the App and stored on the device.
  • Satellite imagery is loaded online from an external service, as described in section 2.
  • Location is optional; you can explore and search parcels without granting location permission.
