This policy describes how the app “Al-Moathen El-Chinguitti” (the “App”) handles data. The App is developed and owned by El Ougl Software SARL (the “Company”, “we”). By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read this policy.

1Data stored locally on your device

The App may store in its private space on your device:

  • the selected city or its coordinates, the prayer-time calculation method, the Asr method, the high-latitude method, time corrections and the Hijri-date correction;
  • the language, font, text size, numeral style, light or dark mode and default calendar;
  • alert modes, selected sounds, randomised daily reminder plans and custom reminder schedules;
  • audio files you select from your phone, which the App copies into its private space for local playback and does not upload to the Company or to any server;
  • cached timetables.

This data stays on your device until you edit or delete it, clear the App’s data or uninstall the App; clearing it or uninstalling may remove it permanently. The Company cannot access this data and does not view it; if it is lost, the Company cannot restore it, as Android backup is disabled for the App.

2External services (separate from local storage)

  • AlAdhan (api.aladhan.com): when timetables are updated online, the App requests the monthly timetable from this service, sending the latitude, longitude, year, month, calculation method, Asr school and high-latitude setting. The service processes the request under its own terms and policy and may see your IP address. If the request fails, the App may use a local calculation or stored data.
  • Android geocoding provider: your device’s system may process the coordinates to convert them into a country or city name, under its provider’s policies.
  • Browser: external links open in the browser and are subject to the policy of the site opened.

These services are subject to their own terms and privacy policies, and we give no warranty as to their accuracy, safety or continuity.

3Location and sensors

Location is optional. If you grant permission, the App requests a recent location to identify the nearest listed city and calculate the Qibla direction, and uses the time zone and elevation when available; it does not track location in the background. If you deny permission, you can choose the country and city manually. The Qibla compass uses your device’s rotation, accelerometer and magnetic-field readings, which are processed only during use and are neither saved nor sent.

4Permissions

  • Internet: timetable updates and external links.
  • Approximate and precise location (optional): nearest city and Qibla.
  • Notifications: prayer notifications and reminders.
  • Exact alarms: attempting to deliver alerts at their scheduled times.
  • Vibration: the vibration alert mode.
  • Boot, time and time-zone events: rescheduling alerts.
  • Foreground media service: playing the adhan or audio with a visible notification.

The App does not request camera, microphone, contacts or messaging access.

5Your choices

You can deny the location permission and choose a city manually, disable notifications, exact alarms or vibration, delete custom sounds and reminders, clear the App’s data or uninstall it. Features tied to a disabled permission may stop working.

6Security, children and international processing

Your data stays on your device, and timetable requests travel over an encrypted connection (HTTPS); no system is completely secure, and you are responsible for protecting your device. The App is general-purpose and not directed at children; a parent or guardian should review the location and notification permissions when a child uses it. External services may process technical data in other countries under their own terms.

7Changes and contact

We may amend this policy when data processing or legal requirements change; the amended text is usually published with its update date. When you write to us, we process your email address, your message and any attachments you send to the extent necessary to handle your request; contacting us creates no obligation to reply or to answer your questions.

8Summary

  • The App works locally on your device and does not send your data to the Company.
  • It requires no account or sign-in.
  • It contains no advertising and no analytics tools.
  • The Company cannot access the settings, alerts and audio files saved in the App and holds no copy of them; it cannot restore them if they are lost.
  • Location is optional; you can choose the country and city manually.
  • The App connects to an external service to retrieve prayer timetables, as described in section 2.
