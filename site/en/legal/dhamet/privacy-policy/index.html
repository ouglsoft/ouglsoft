This policy describes how the game “Dhamet” (the “Game”) handles data. The Game is developed, owned and operated by El Ougl Software SARL (the “Company”, “we”). By using the Game, you acknowledge that you have read this policy.

1Data processed by the servers and purposes

1.1 Account and authentication (registered accounts): email address; password (stored as a salted, non-reversible hash); Google account identifier if you choose that sign-in method; an internal account identifier; nickname; avatar; account creation date and last-activity date; sign-in session records (hashed session token, validity period, IP address and browser identifier at the time the session is created). Purposes: creating the account, signing in, password recovery, and protecting the account and the service.

1.2 Guest mode: a technical identifier, nickname, avatar and sign-in session, without an email address. No statistics or ranking are recorded for a guest.

1.3 Profile and ranking (registered accounts): nickname, avatar, match results and statistics (wins, losses, draws, points) and ranking. Your nickname, avatar, points and rank are visible to other users on the leaderboard. For matches against the computer, the result and brief related information (opponent level, player side, ending reason, timestamp) are sent, without the move history.

1.4 Online play: presence and connection status, invitations sent and received, rooms and matches (board, turns, moves, match status), and spectating. Your nickname, avatar and status are visible to other users within the Game.

1.5 Text chat: message text, sender identifier, nickname and role (player or spectator), and time sent; these are visible to the participants in the room.

1.6 Voice: an optional feature that requires microphone permission in the browser. Audio travels in real time through WebRTC between participants, directly or through relay servers. The Company does not record or retain audio; the servers process temporary technical signalling data to establish the connection. Other participants and the connection services listed in section 4 may see your IP address.

1.7 Connection and security: IP address and browser information when requests are processed, and short-lived technical counters linked to a hashed network-address identifier to limit request rates and prevent abuse.

1.8 Email: the password-recovery message is sent to your address through an external email-delivery provider.

2Data stored on your device

The Game stores in your browser the language, appearance and game settings, the saved match against the computer so you can resume it, the identifier of the last active match, invitation preferences, and the basic account and session data needed to run the interface. The Game uses a session cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days for registered accounts and expires when the browser is closed for a guest. Some of this data is removed when you close the tab or browser; some remains until you delete it or your browser clears it.

The Company cannot access the Game’s settings and preferences or the saved match against the computer on your device, and does not view them; if they are lost (for example by clearing browser data or changing device), the Company cannot restore them. Account data processed by the servers is described in section 1.

3Google Analytics

The Game uses Google Analytics to measure usage and improve performance, and it runs automatically when the Game is opened. Google may process technical identifiers, cookies, device and browser data, and general events such as the start, end, mode and duration of play, under its policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can limit this through your browser settings or tracking-protection tools.

4External services

The Game relies on the following external services as needed:

  • Cloudflare: hosting, servers, databases and protection.
  • Google: sign-in with a Google account when you choose it, Google Analytics, and a STUN service for voice connections.
  • Resend: sending emails.
  • Cloudflare and ExpressTURN: voice connection relay services.
  • Google Firebase: infrastructure for the backup service described in section 5.

These services are subject to their own terms and privacy policies and may process data in countries other than yours. We give no warranty as to their accuracy, safety or continuity.

5Backup service

To keep the service available, your play session may be temporarily redirected to an official backup address under the Company’s domains that runs on Google Firebase infrastructure; this policy applies to data processed through it.

6Data retention

  • Account and profile data, results, statistics, points and ranking: for as long as the account exists.
  • Sign-in sessions: their validity ends after a maximum of 30 days, and their records are deleted on sign-out, account deletion or password change.
  • Presence and invitations: temporary data that disappears on disconnection or expiry.
  • Matches, chat, spectating and voice signalling: temporary operational data deleted automatically after a match ends or is abandoned or declined, within a short period that ordinarily does not exceed two days.
  • Data stored on your device: until you delete it or your browser clears it.
  • Emails sent to us: for as long as needed to handle them.
  • Google Analytics and external provider data: according to their settings and policies.

7Account deletion

You can delete your account from within the Game at any time; this requires you to confirm your identity again. As soon as deletion is complete, all data linked to your account is permanently deleted, namely:

  • account and profile data: email address, nickname, avatar and Google identifier;
  • sign-in sessions;
  • results and statistics;
  • points and ranking;
  • invitations and presence status, and any other records linked to your account.

We do not retain any result or statistic relating to you after deletion. Chat messages you sent in matches linked to your account are deleted, and your name is replaced with “Deleted user” in any temporary match record not yet deleted, which is then deleted automatically as set out in section 6. The system keeps an internal, deactivated technical record that contains no email address, nickname, avatar or Google identifier, and no results or statistics. The account cannot be restored after deletion. Deletion does not extend to data retained by external providers under their own policies, including Google Analytics data and their operational logs.

8Your choices and rights

  • Edit your nickname, avatar, email address and password from within the Game.
  • Use the Game as a guest without an email address.
  • Turn off invitation requests, avoid chat and voice, and revoke microphone permission in the browser.
  • Clear the browser data stored on your device.

9Chat and content

Chat messages are visible to the participants in the room, so do not share private or sensitive information. You are responsible for what you write or say in chat and voice.

10Children

The Game is a general-audience game and is not specifically directed at children. A person who has not reached the legal age in their country must use it with the consent of a parent or legal guardian.

11Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, and data travels between the Game and its servers over an encrypted connection. No system is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your sign-in credentials and your device and for not sharing your account.

12International processing

Data may be processed by providers in countries other than yours. By using the Game, you acknowledge this.

13Changes and contact

We may amend this policy when data processing or legal requirements change; the amended text is usually published with its update date. When you write to us, we process your email address, your message and any attachments you send to the extent necessary to handle your request; contacting us creates no obligation to reply or to answer your questions.

14Summary

  • Play against the computer runs inside your browser, on your device; the Company cannot access the saved match or the Game settings stored on your device.
  • Account and online-play features (invitations, matches, spectating, chat, voice, leaderboard) require data processing on servers operated by the Company through cloud infrastructure providers.
  • You may use the Game as a guest without an email address; statistics and ranking are limited to registered accounts.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • The Game uses Google Analytics automatically to measure usage.
  • You can delete your account from within the Game.
