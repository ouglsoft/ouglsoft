This policy describes how the app “My Agenda” (the “App”) handles data. The App is developed and owned by El Ougl Software SARL (the “Company”, “we”). By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read this policy.

1Data you enter

The data you enter may include the titles of activities, tasks and appointments, places you type manually, notes, categories, targets, units of measurement, dates, times, progress and make-up records, reminders and settings. Depending on what you enter, this data may be personal or sensitive; you remain in control of it.

This data is stored on your device; the Company cannot access it and does not view it. If it is lost (for example by uninstalling the App, clearing its data or a device failure), the Company cannot restore it.

2Purposes of processing

This data is processed on your device to display the agenda and activities, calculate progress and reports, schedule reminders, display the widget, manage categories and the archive, and provide backup and restore. It is not used for advertising, tracking or marketing profiles.

3Local storage and automatic copies

The App saves data as JSON in its private Android storage. It keeps a previous copy and up to seven automatic daily copies, and may keep a copy before a data-format migration. The App does not encrypt these files itself; their protection depends on Android and your device. The data stays until you change or delete it, clear the App’s data or uninstall the App, which may remove it permanently. The Company cannot access these files and does not view them, and has no means of restoring them.

4Export, import and backup

  • You can export a JSON backup or a CSV log, and choose the destination or restore source through the system file picker, including external storage services if you choose them; those services are subject to their own terms and policies.
  • The App does not send these files to a server operated by the Company. Exported files may contain readable information, so store them securely.
  • Restoring a backup may replace the current data.
  • Android backup and data transfer are enabled for the App; its data (the agenda file and its automatic copies) may be copied according to your device settings and the service you choose, and its policy.

5Permissions and reminders

The App uses the permissions for notifications, exact alarms and rescheduling reminders after the device starts; Android may require additional permissions or settings. Notifications and the widget may display activity titles and times on screen, including the lock screen depending on your device settings. You can control notifications from the App and from Android settings. The App does not request location, camera, microphone or contacts access.

6Sharing and third parties

The Company does not sell your agenda data and does not receive it. Data may reach a recipient you choose when you use system backup, export or sharing, whose policy then applies. Visiting the Company’s website or contacting it is separate from the local agenda and is governed by the website’s privacy policy.

7Your choices

You can view, correct, export and delete your data within the App, disable notifications, delete activities and records, clear the App’s data or uninstall it, and delete external copies yourself. Archiving is not deletion. Local copies, exported files and system backups may retain earlier content depending on backup cycles, device settings and storage destinations.

8Security and children

Protect your device and restrict access to backups; no security measure guarantees absolute protection. The App is not directed at children, and a parent or guardian should supervise where necessary. Do not enter other people’s data without their permission or a legal basis.

9Changes and contact

We may amend this policy when data processing or legal requirements change; the amended text is usually published with its update date. When you write to us, we process your email address, your message and any attachments you send to the extent necessary to handle your request; contacting us creates no obligation to reply or to answer your questions.

10Summary

  • The App works locally on your device and does not send the content of your agenda or your progress records to the Company.
  • It requires no account or sign-in, does not request the Internet permission and does not connect to any server.
  • The Company cannot access or view your data and holds no copy of it; it cannot restore your data if it is lost.
  • It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • It does not request location, camera, microphone or contacts access.
