This policy describes how the app “Zad Al-Muslim” (the “App”) handles data. The App is developed and owned by El Ougl Software SARL (the “Company”, “we”). By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read this policy.

1Data stored locally on your device

The App may store in its private space on your device: the selected city, its coordinates, elevation and time zone; prayer-time calculation settings and corrections; language, appearance, font, numeral and calendar preferences; cached timetables; favourites and pinned shortcuts; the last content used and usage counters; smart-tasbih counts and targets; Qur’an goals, page ranges and reading progress; Ramadan make-up days; personal worship-plan items (name, note, repeat, time); zakat tracking entries (name, type, amount, due date, recipient, notes, payment status); and the record of deeds completed in the daily checklist (date, item).

This data stays on your device until you edit or delete it, clear the App’s data or uninstall the App; clearing it or uninstalling may remove it permanently. The Company cannot access this data and does not view it; if it is lost, the Company cannot restore it, as Android backup is disabled for the App. The App’s private storage does not protect it from someone who has access to your device.

2External services (separate from local storage)

  • AlAdhan (api.aladhan.com): to refresh the monthly timetable, the App requests it from this service, sending the coordinates of the selected city or determined location, the year and month, the calculation method, the Asr school and the high-latitude setting. The service processes the request under its own terms and policy and may see your IP address. If the request fails, the App may use stored data or a local calculation.
  • Android geocoding provider: if you allow location, your device’s system may process the coordinates to determine a city name, under its provider’s policies.
  • Browser: external links open in the browser and are subject to the policy of the site opened.

These services are subject to their own terms and privacy policies, and we give no warranty as to their accuracy, safety or continuity.

3Location and sensors

Location is optional. If you grant permission, the App may request an approximate or precise location to identify the city, the Qibla direction and the prayer-time parameters; it does not request location in the background. If you deny permission, you can choose the city manually, and features tied to the permission may be limited. The Qibla relies on your device’s rotation, accelerometer and magnetic-field readings, which are processed only while the feature is open and are neither saved nor sent.

4Permissions

  • Internet: timetable updates and external links.
  • Approximate and precise location (optional): identifying the city, the Qibla and prayer-time parameters.
  • Vibration: haptic feedback for the smart tasbih and related interactions.

The App does not request camera, microphone, contacts or messaging access.

5Your choices

You can deny the location permission and choose a city manually, edit or delete your local plans and records, clear the App’s data or uninstall it.

6Security, children and international processing

Your data stays on your device, and timetable requests travel over an encrypted connection (HTTPS); no system is completely secure, and you are responsible for protecting your device. The App is general-purpose and not directed at children; a parent or guardian should review the location permission when a child uses it. External services may process technical data in other countries under their own terms.

7Changes and contact

We may amend this policy when data processing or legal requirements change; the amended text is usually published with its update date. When you write to us, we process your email address, your message and any attachments you send to the extent necessary to handle your request; contacting us creates no obligation to reply or to answer your questions.

8Summary

  • The App works locally on your device and does not send your data to the Company.
  • It requires no account or sign-in.
  • It contains no advertising and no analytics tools.
  • The Company cannot access the data saved in the App and holds no copy of it; it cannot restore the data if it is lost.
  • The App connects to an external service to retrieve prayer timetables, as described in section 2.
