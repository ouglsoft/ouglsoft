This policy describes how the website ouglsoft.com (the “Website”) handles data. The Website is developed and owned by El Ougl Software SARL (the “Company”, “we”). By using the Website, you acknowledge that you have read this policy.

1Hosting and security

Cloudflare serves the Website’s pages, files, downloads and functions and may process IP address, request, browser and device data, timing and security signals to deliver and protect content and handle incidents; it may keep operational and security logs depending on its settings. Downloads from the Website are subject to this technical processing.

2Google Analytics

The Website uses Google Analytics to measure visits and improve its content and performance; it operates automatically when you browse pages. The data processed may include cookie identifiers, browser and device information, and the pages you visit. Google signals and ad personalisation are disabled, and the referring page address is not sent. You can limit this processing by blocking cookies or measurement tools in your browser or with the opt-out add-on provided by Google. Google processes the data under its policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

3Google Fonts

The Website’s pages use Google Fonts: your browser contacts the font provider, which may receive the IP address and browser and request data. See: policies.google.com/privacy.

4Contact form

If you use the form, the Website receives your name, email address, subject, message, page language and path, and anti-bot verification data (a hidden field and start time). Name, email, subject and message are required to submit; you can email us directly instead. The message passes through the Resend service to the Company’s mailbox and authorised recipients; the form does not create a separate message database. Messages may remain in mailboxes and provider logs depending on their settings and retention requirements. See: resend.com/legal/privacy-policy.

5Correspondence and requests

When you write to us or send a report or attachment, we receive your email address, your message, your files and the technical data accompanying them, and use them to handle your request, protect security and meet legal requirements. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. We keep correspondence as long as needed for the request, disputes and statutory periods.

6Third parties

The external services mentioned are subject to their own terms and policies and may process data in other countries depending on their infrastructure. The Company does not control the practices, failures or changes of these independent third parties. We may disclose data we hold where the law requires or to protect rights and security within legal limits.

7Changes and contact

We may amend this policy when data processing or legal requirements change; the amended text is usually published with its update date. When you write to us, we process your email address, your message and any attachments you send to the extent necessary to handle your request; contacting us creates no obligation to reply or to answer your questions.

8Summary

  • Cloudflare hosts the Website’s pages and downloads and may process technical data when you browse it.
  • The Website uses Google Analytics to measure visits, with Google signals and ad personalisation disabled.
  • The Website requires no account or sign-in and contains no advertising.
  • Contact-form messages pass through Resend to the Company’s mailbox; the form does not create a separate message database.
  • Each app and service has its own privacy policy, which governs the processing of its data.
