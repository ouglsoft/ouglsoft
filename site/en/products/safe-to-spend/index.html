About the app

Safe-to-Spend

Safe-to-Spend helps you manage a personal budget by recording income, expenses and transfers and comparing them with spending goals. The home screen shows the amount remaining based on your budget, your spending pace and the projected monthly expenses. Records stay on your phone, with no bank account to link.

Features

Remaining spending amount and daily spending pace.
Budget goals, monthly projections and comparisons with past months and the annual average.
Income, expenses, transfers, account groups and categories.
Category budgets, and the option to exclude selected transactions from calculations.
Daily, calendar and monthly views, notes, favourites and trash.
Backup, CSV/TSV/TXT import and export, operation history and optional app lock.

Inside the app

Good to know

Estimates, not advice

Forecasts and comparisons are based on what you record. They are indicative and are not financial advice.

Back up your data

Records are stored on your phone. Back up and export regularly: clearing app data or uninstalling may remove them permanently.

Optional app lock

You can protect the app with an optional lock. Local storage does not protect your data from someone who has access to your unlocked device.
