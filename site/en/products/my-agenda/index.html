About the app

My Agenda

My Agenda goes beyond a task list: seven activity types let you choose the nature of a commitment and how to measure it. The home screen highlights priorities, overdue items and completed work, and the agenda offers daily, weekly and monthly views. One entry contributes to goals across overlapping periods, while changes to future plans preserve earlier records. Reports distinguish completed commitments from average actual progress, and help you review past days and make up missed work.

Features

Seven types: tasks, appointments, regular activities, flexible activities, goals, timed commitments and make-up plans.
Daily, weekly and monthly views, progress rings and automatic status colours.
Multi-period targets, measured by completion, count, quantity, duration or time, with remaining work and required pace.
Categories and subcategories, search, filters, sorting and per-activity history.
Overall, category and activity reports for daily to yearly or custom periods.
Future plan changes that preserve past records; day exclusions, pauses, archiving and record corrections.
Make-up plans for missed commitments, linked to the original activity.
Local reminders, quiet hours, periodic summaries and a home-screen widget.
Local storage, automatic backups, restore and JSON/CSV export, in light or dark appearance.

Good to know

Local data

Your agenda is stored on your phone. Use the built-in backup and export to keep a copy: clearing app data or uninstalling may remove local data.

Reminders

Reminders and quiet hours depend on your phone’s notification and battery settings.

Languages

My Agenda is available in Arabic and English.
