About the app

Nouakchott Land Parcel Map

The app lets you search for parcels by district, neighbourhood or plan, sector and parcel number, or select them directly on the map. Parcel data is stored on your device, while satellite imagery is loaded online. Save locations and share their coordinates.

Features

Search by district, neighbourhood or plan, sector and parcel number.
View parcel boundaries and select one or several parcels.
See the available data, including area and coordinates.
Show your position on the map, only when you ask for it.
Save favourites, copy and share parcel details.
Esri satellite imagery when connected.
Adjust imagery style, parcel shading and multi-selection options.

Inside the app

Good to know

For information only

Parcel data is provided for information and reference. It is not an official land registry or a certified survey: verify title, boundaries and area with the competent authorities before any transaction.

Imagery needs a connection

Satellite imagery is loaded from Esri over the internet. The parcel database itself is stored on your device.

Local data

Your settings and favourites stay on your device. The app has no account and no advertising.
