About the game

Dhamet

Dhamet brings the traditional Mauritanian game to your browser. Learn the rules, practise against the computer at your own level, or challenge other players online, and follow your results and rankings with an account.

Features

Play against the computer at different difficulty levels.
Online matches, invitations and spectator options where available.
Guest access, or sign-in by email or Google.
Move history, results and rankings with an account.
Save a local game and resume it in the same browser.
Text and voice chat where supported.

Inside the game

Good to know

Online play

Matches, invitations, chat and voice need an internet connection and run on servers operated by El Ougl Software SARL.

Accounts

You can play as a guest. Statistics and rankings are available to registered accounts, and you can delete your account from within the game.

Local games

Games against the computer run in your browser and can be saved and resumed in the same browser.

Play in your browser

Dhamet runs in your browser — nothing to install.
