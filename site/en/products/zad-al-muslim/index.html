About the app

Zad Al-Muslim

Zad Al-Muslim brings together the Quran, reading plans, completion tracking, remembrance and supplications, alongside everyday worship tools. Save favourites, track your progress and set a personal worship plan. Core content works offline, without an account.

Features

Quran reading, daily reading plans and completion tracking.
Remembrance and supplications, with search and favourites.
Prayer times, Qibla, Hijri calendar and night times.
Tasbih counter and zakat calculators.
Daily worship plan and content for Islamic seasons.
A knowledge library: the Names of Allah, concise rulings, virtues and etiquette.
Progress and settings saved on your phone.
