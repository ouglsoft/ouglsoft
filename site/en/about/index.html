About OuglSoft

Who we are

OuglSoft is the brand of El Ougl Software SARL, a Mauritanian company specialising in software development and digital products.

We design and develop mobile apps, websites, online platforms and technical tools, for ourselves and for our clients, in whatever field the need calls for. Each project takes its shape from the need it addresses.

Whatever the sector or the size of the project, we start by understanding the need and the people who will use the software, then design, build and deliver a product that is clear, dependable and easy to maintain.

Our vision

We aim to build software that does its job clearly and remains maintainable and adaptable over time.

Our approach

We analyse each request fully and define the scope and the vision before starting. We care about design, usability, consistent interfaces and performance, build with craftsmanship, and stand by our commitments. Technologies and languages are chosen to suit each project.

Local perspective

We take users’ languages, habits and ways of using technology into account. We are based in Mauritania and adapt each product to the people it is intended for.
