FAQ

Before we start

What do you need from me to start?

A short description of the need: who will use the product, what it should do, and any deadline or existing material. We refine the scope together before development starts.

Can you improve a product that already exists?

Yes. We can review an existing app or website, propose priorities, and either improve it step by step or rebuild the parts that hold it back.

Which languages do you work in?

Arabic, French and English. Interfaces are built for right-to-left and left-to-right reading from the start.

How do we get in touch?

Write to contact@ouglsoft.com or use the contact form, and tell us briefly what you have in mind.

Have a project in mind?

Tell us what you need — a new app, a website, or improvements to an existing product.
